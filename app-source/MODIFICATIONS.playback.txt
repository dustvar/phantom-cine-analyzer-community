Compact Workspace 1.11.0 — 2026-10-27

  preserving Video Overlay and old comparator job settings. Active Cine title-bar
  controls replace the floating tracking toolbar; narrow panes use a More menu.

  Add Object setup and separated Report from Viewer Export button connections.

  references for all methods, with saved-job persistence and point picking.

  Old jobs recalculate derived metrics without retracking stored points.

Native Startup 1.10.1 — 2026-10-27

- Added direct native Qt startup, a visible splash, startup diagnostics and
  single-instance document forwarding. Finder can open Cine and saved jobs.

Sequence Editor 1.7.0 — 2026-09-14

- Added side-by-side Multiply Video and Add New Video controls. Each addition
  is a fresh, independent source pass. Cine and ordinary video sources can be
  sequenced; video labels and cyan chain links distinguish source joins from
  regular cuts. Joins retain speed-transition controls but their boundaries
  stay fixed. Viewer frame indices remain separate from tracking indices.
- Added timeline magnification with minus/plus, scrolling, and Fit.
- Added segment zoom presets/custom factors, an adjustable yellow crop frame,
  and timed zoom-in/out easing. Plain splits preserve existing zoom motion.
- Added timed custom text with position, duration, size, color, background,
  and independent zoom anchoring. Data boxes can also stay on screen or move
  with the footage. The source guide is excluded from exports.
- Updated recipe migration and source validation for multi-video edits.
  Export shares preview timing/crops/text, fits differing aspect ratios, and
  remains silent. See docs/VIEWER_SEQUENCE.md for workflow and limits.

Data & Cut Editing 1.6.0 — 2026-09-14

- Include Data now has Playback Data and Camera Data selectors, each with its
  own Enable, fields, layout, decimals, size, color, background, and position.
  Playback defaults to slowdown/playback FPS at bottom right; capture FPS is
  optional and off by default. Camera defaults to model/capture FPS/resolution
  at bottom left, using the selected Cine's metadata. Both boxes are draggable
  and share preview/export rendering; recipes and undo preserve both settings.
- Transition dialogs have an editable Cut at frame number and linked slider,
  using the Cine's frame numbering, including negative pretrigger frames.
  Dragging a junction previews the boundary and records one undo step on release.
  Each neighbor retains at least one source frame. Escape cancels a drag.
- Added Undo and Redo immediately beside the source timeline. Existing section
  playback rates, transition settings, and other cut locations survive cut moves.

Data Layouts 1.5.1 — 2026-09-14

  Whole-number display is the default. Formatting does not change timing.

Transitions & Data 1.5.0 — 2026-09-14

  docs/VIEWER_EDITOR.md for usage and verification details.

  confidence also accounts for edge-map saturation, edge overlap, peak
  uniqueness, and motion continuity so dense background noise is not reported
  as a high-quality lock. Smart processing also leaves the user-confirmed
  anchor unscored instead of reporting a meaningless perfect self-match. It

1.12.1 · Responsive playback
- Read a bounded window of frames with an independent reader. Reuse the loaded
  Cine index; avoid rereading frame zero after a seek. Late reads cannot publish
  frames after pause or a source change. Retain original timeline/export sampling.
- Use clock deadlines for presentation, and keep hidden plots out of the playback path.
- Cache pixel-identical monochrome tone conversion when camera settings permit.

1.13.0 · Background-Oriented Schlieren
- Measure local subpixel dot movement relative to a pinned reference in a drawn rectangle.
  Use local contrast normalization, forward/backward agreement and patch correlation.
- View signed horizontal or vertical displacement and magnitude on a fixed pixel
  scale. Retain the original image at unsupported locations.
- Save and relink the analysis in whole-job files; export the displayed overlay
  as a PNG or MP4. A separate reader streams the chosen frame range.

1.13.1 · BOS fine detail
- Normalize dot contrast in raw sensor precision before optical-flow calculations.
  Bright features in one part of the ROI no longer suppress clear dots in another.
- Finer sampling, optional stationary reference averaging, and point-by-point mask
  explanations are available in BOS. New jobs remember those choices.

1.14.0 · Viewer Studio

  lower brackets or use Mark In and Mark Out to mark the displayed frame.

1.14.1 · BOS measurement methods and overlay noise reduction

  original method and appearance. BOS jobs and exports remember both choices.
